# Asphalt microtexture

Use case: stylized-concept  
Asset type: tileable game texture  
Primary request: seamless neutral graphite asphalt microtexture viewed perfectly top-down  
Style/medium: realistic but restrained game texture; fine aggregate, subtle repaired grain and tiny tonal variation  
Composition/framing: square orthographic texture; uniform density; edges must tile seamlessly in both axes; no dominant crack or focal mark  
Lighting/mood: flat diffuse material reference without directional shadows or highlights  
Color palette: dark neutral graphite and charcoal only  
Constraints: no lane markings; no tire marks; no debris; no oil stains; no text; no logos; no watermark; no border; seamless edges
Avoid: perspective, road scene, curbs, gravel chunks, dramatic wet reflections

Approval: adds material detail at 6–12% opacity without competing with projected lane geometry.

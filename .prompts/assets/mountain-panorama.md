# Mountain panorama

Use case: stylized-concept  
Asset type: transparent game landscape layer  
Primary request: original continuous panorama of distant low mountains and sparse conifer ridges for a fast highway landscape  
Scene/backdrop: perfectly flat solid #ff00ff chroma-key background for local removal; uniform color only; no sky gradient or ground plane  
Subject: one uninterrupted layered mountain silhouette, cool slate-blue and charcoal-green, restrained atmospheric detail, no individual focal peak  
Style/medium: cinematic matte-painting silhouette with simplified realistic forms, compatible with Canvas tinting  
Composition/framing: very wide horizontal band; mountains occupy the lower 48% of the canvas; transparent-intent sky area above; edges designed to overlap when repeated  
Lighting/mood: neutral diffuse light without fixed sun direction so code-driven day/night grading remains believable  
Constraints: no road; no buildings; no people; no vehicles; no text; no logo; no watermark; no shadows on background; do not use #ff00ff in the subject
Avoid: famous landmarks, dramatic single peak, photographic sky, hard horizon rectangle

Approval: ridge remains legible after tinting and downscaling to 1280 × 180.
